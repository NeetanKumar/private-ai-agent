# Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over and must be used by March 31 of the following year.

## Sick leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A doctor's note is required after 3 consecutive sick days.

## Parental leave
The primary caregiver receives 16 weeks of paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver receives 6 weeks. Leave may be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## Other leave
Bereavement leave is 5 days for immediate family. After 7 years of service, employees may request an unpaid sabbatical of up to 8 weeks. The company observes 11 public holidays each year.
