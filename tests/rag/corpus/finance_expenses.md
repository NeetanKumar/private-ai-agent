# Travel and Expense Policy

## Limits
Meals are reimbursed up to $60 per day while travelling. Hotels are capped at $220 per night. Receipts are required for any expense over $25.

## Flights
Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer.

## Approvals
Managers approve expenses up to $1,000. Directors approve $1,000 to $10,000. Anything over $10,000 needs CFO approval.

## Reimbursement
Approved expenses are reimbursed within 14 days. Corporate card statements must be reconciled by the 5th of each month. The mileage rate is $0.62 per mile.
