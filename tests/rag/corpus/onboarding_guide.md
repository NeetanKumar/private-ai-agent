# New Hire Onboarding

## First day
Arrive at 9:30 and collect your badge at reception. Your laptop is delivered before your start date.

## First weeks
Every new hire is paired with a buddy for 90 days. Mandatory security training must be completed within the first week. Benefits enrollment closes 30 days after the start date.

## Setup
A one-time home office stipend of $500 is available. The probation period is 6 months.
