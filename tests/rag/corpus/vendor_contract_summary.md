# Hosting Vendor Contract Summary

The agreement runs for 36 months starting 2026-01-01 and renews automatically for 12 months unless cancelled.

## Fees and service levels
The monthly fee is $18,500. The uptime SLA is 99.95%. For each 0.1% shortfall the vendor issues a service credit of 10% of the monthly fee.

## Exit terms
Termination requires 90 days written notice. After termination the vendor returns customer data within 30 days. Liability is capped at 12 months of fees.
